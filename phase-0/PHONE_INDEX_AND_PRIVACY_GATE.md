# Cliniko phone index and patient-data boundary

**Status:** Phone-index storage, synthetic full scan, periodic refresh worker, and live candidate recheck are implemented and locally tested. The full receptionist flow is not yet compliant. This contract supersedes earlier proposals for retained transcripts, recordings, patient-specific presentation text, action payloads, and patient-linked call history in Abby.

## Storage rule

The only patient information Abby may persist is a Cliniko patient ID and a patient phone number. The allowed relation is `(tenant, clinic, normalizedPhone, clinikoPatientId)`. A phone can map to multiple patient IDs, and one patient can have multiple phones. Tenant and clinic are routing scope, not evidence of caller identity. The index has no name, date of birth, email, appointment, call ID, outcome, note, recording, transcript, or free-text column. No derived hash, ciphertext, trace, error, cache, backup, archive, or log may carry a prohibited patient value. Phone numbers and IDs have explicit deletion and rebuild paths. Generic clinic knowledge and settings must be checked for patient-specific content before persistence.

The call path receives the authenticated transport's presented caller number. The connected voice call exposes a transient candidate lookup only after its clinic location is bound; the number is not copied into its durable admission, call state, or event payload. It normalizes the number with the clinic's configured country/number plan and queries the local index for that clinic. Unparseable, withheld, or ambiguous numbers yield no automatic patient selection. Candidate IDs are re-read from Cliniko at call time and accepted as candidates only if the returned, non-archived record still has the presented phone. The re-read data stays transient. A caller ID match is never identity verification; a shared number must not disclose one patient's appointment to another caller. Clinic-approved verification and exact host authorization are still required before appointment reads or writes.

## Sync and freshness

[Cliniko's patient API](https://docs.api.cliniko.com/openapi/patient) documents `patient_phone_numbers` in patient records but does not list phone among the `GET /patients` filters. The adapter must page through patient records outside the call path, extracting only `id` and `patient_phone_numbers` into a staged clinic-scoped index. It discards every other response field immediately. A complete scan atomically replaces the published generation; partial pages, malformed records, duplicates that violate schema, 429 responses, unavailable pages, or worker loss leave the previous generation marked stale, never partly published. A resumable checkpoint contains only page position, generation, and non-patient operational counters. The rate budget respects Cliniko's response headers and bounded retries. Incremental `updated_at` reads may reduce lag, but periodic full reconciliation is required to catch removals, archives, merges, and changed numbers. No live call starts a whole-clinic scan.

The published generation records completion time and source scope separately from the phone/ID rows. Readiness requires a complete generation within an approved freshness window. A stale or missing generation blocks automatic patient lookup and routes to bounded clarification or staff. Even a fresh generation supplies candidates only: the live Cliniko read rejects stale phone links and archived records. If Cliniko is unavailable, Abby does not infer a patient from the local index alone.

The standalone `npm run phone-index:refresh` worker takes `DATABASE_URL`, `ABBY_TENANT_ID`, `ABBY_CLINIKO_API_KEY`, `ABBY_CLINIKO_CONTACT_EMAIL`, and `ABBY_COUNTRY_CALLING_CODE` from its environment, plus `ABBY_CLINIC_ID` to scope one clinic. Optional `ABBY_PHONE_REFRESH_EVERY_MS` and `ABBY_PHONE_REFRESH_POLL_MS` control the interval. It scans at startup when no fresh generation exists, then polls for due refreshes; results log only `fresh`, `refreshed`, or `failed`. The scan resumes the same page after a local budget reset or provider 429, with at most twelve bounded waits. It accepts up to 100,000 patient records and 100,000 unique phone-to-ID links; larger snapshots fail closed. A failed scan marks its starting generation stale while preserving its rows; a newer concurrent publication remains fresh, and an older scan cannot replace it. Run one worker per clinic to avoid duplicated Cliniko reads. A process restart starts a complete scan again. Database-coordinated worker ownership and resumable page checkpoints remain required before multi-replica deployment or large-clinic readiness.

Normalization must be deterministic and conservative. Use the clinic's country plan, preserve a canonical international representation, and reject numbers that cannot be unambiguously normalized. Do not match by suffix, strip arbitrary leading digits, or collapse extensions into a patient phone. Only documented patient phone fields enter the index; fax and other non-calling numbers require an explicit product rule before matching. A fixture covers Australian local and international forms, shared family numbers, missing caller ID, extensions, changed numbers, archive, and two clinics with the same number.

## Durable action boundary

Patient and appointment details needed for a live call may exist in process memory but not Abby persistence. A host-owned command/task service must own the durable patient-specific request, appointment target or offer, exact-key reconciliation, and staff follow-up. Abby may hold a random, non-reversible command reference only while it cannot expose a patient-linked outcome through that reference or its surrounding call metadata. The storage serializer is an explicit allowlist and rejects unexpected fields. If the host cannot own and reconcile the patient-specific operation, automation is disabled and Abby makes a generic staff handoff without patient-specific notes or a claim that a booking was submitted.

Before any real patient traffic, an E2E test must drive a phone-index refresh, candidate lookup, shared-number verification failure, live Cliniko re-read, patient-specific action, retry/crash, staff handoff, call review, export, logs, and backup fixture. It scans every Abby durable surface for canary names, dates, appointment IDs/times, request text, and patient-linked outcomes. Every new feature or setting toggle extends that E2E with its new persistence path. A test that merely checks a redacted UI is insufficient.

## Current code gap

The existing PostgreSQL `calls.state`, `effect_outbox.payload`, and `staff_items.payload` serialize rich call/action state. A disposable PostgreSQL journey now confirms exact synthetic offer references in `calls.state` and `effect_outbox.payload`, and an appointment reference in `calls.state` and `staff_items.payload`. The database-wide canary scanner returns only table and column names and sees no final caller-turn text in that journey; it is diagnostic evidence of the violation, not a privacy pass or a substitute for an allowlisted serializer, logs, backups, and archive checks. Standalone booking, cancellation, rescheduling, and intake now default to `unavailable`; an E2E checks that proposed offer, appointment, and intake canaries never reach PostgreSQL under that profile. Explicit synthetic `requestOnly` and `execute` profiles still exercise the unsafe legacy state until a host-owned command/task seam replaces it. Caller-turn text digests have been removed from new states and scrubbed from legacy rows; replay now uses the stable turn ID without storing a speech-derived hash. The phone index, synthetic full scan, periodic single-process refresh worker, and post-binding voice lookup port exist, but there is no crash-resumable refresh, versioned clinic number plan, real telephony adapter supplying presented caller ID, dialogue-level use of candidates, or proven host-owned action seam. The 100,000-patient and 100,000-link scan limits still fail closed for larger clinics. Therefore the current code does **not** meet this storage rule and must not be described as privacy compliant or enabled for real patient calls. The next implementation slice is a privacy-safe ledger/host-action seam, multi-worker refresh ownership, and the full E2E storage gate. Configuration-only archive and synthetic call tests do not close this gap.
