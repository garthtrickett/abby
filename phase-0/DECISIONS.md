# Phase 0 product decisions

**Status:** Product owner fixed the D-06 storage boundary, confirmed phone-to-ID lookup in D-01, and chose Cliniko as the sole durable home for standalone patient-specific work in D-11. On 2026-09-29, the product owner kept patient-specific handoff disabled for the first Cliniko-only release until a clinic approves the workflow. Clinic verification and the actual Cliniko staff workflow still require their listed owners and proof.

| Decision ID | Question | Proposed default pending approval | Owner and deadline |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| D-01 | How is an existing caller verified before appointment details or changes? | The caller's presented phone is looked up in Abby's clinic-scoped phone-to-Cliniko-patient-ID index, then each candidate is re-read from Cliniko. A match is never proof; shared or stale numbers require bounded clarification or staff handoff. Clinic-approved proof is required before protected disclosure or mutation. | Product and clinic owner; before patient lookup automation. |
| D-02 | Which action result may Abby describe as confirmed? | A committed receipt under the host’s declared contract: an atomic exact receipt for an owning diary, or a clear successful Cliniko response plus required exact read-back/conflict inspection under the accepted best-effort contract. Matching later state alone does not prove the original writer or fee/notification effects. Unknown writes remain unresolved; no blind replay. | Product owner accepted standalone best-effort on 2026-10-04 (D-16); clinical activation still needs its own proof. |
| D-03 | What may a first-time caller complete automatically? | Staff request until approved intake consent, duplicate check, stable patient ref, and safe write recovery are proven. | Product and clinic owner; before intake automation. |
| D-04 | Can Abby reschedule an appointment? | Use one in-place move under the approved host contract: atomic source/destination protection for an owning diary, or exact validation and post-write observation under standalone best-effort. Never cancel/create or automatically compensate an unknown/conflicting move. Cliniko-owned review and actual clinic/caller authority remain required before standalone activation. | Product owner selected best-effort 2026-10-04 (D-16); current caller mode remains unavailable pending proof. |
| D-05 | Who owns confirmations and reminders? | One host-approved owner per notification kind, captured for each call; no duplicate send. | Product and clinic owner; before notification implementation. |
| D-06 | What patient information may Abby persist? | Only Cliniko patient IDs and patient phone numbers, in the local lookup index. Audio, transcripts, appointment data, patient-linked outcomes, and free-text notes remain outside Abby persistence. | Decided by product owner; privacy-safe ledger and host handoff required before real patient traffic. |
| D-07 | Where does failed or overdue staff work go? | Patient-specific handoff stays disabled in the first Cliniko-only release until a clinic approves a workflow. A dedicated Cliniko recall type and manually reviewed Recalls report remain a candidate, not an approved queue. No recall POST counts as assignment, notification, acknowledgment, or safe retry. A required operator review route and failure escalation must be proven before enabling handoff. | Product owner decision 2026-09-29; clinic owner approval and workflow proof remain open. |
| D-08 | Which market, locale, and quality thresholds govern the first pilot? | Use existing clinic markets; declare sample size, critical failures, latency and language thresholds before pilot traffic. | Product; before pilot design approval. |
| D-09 | Which telephony route handles failed intake or voice startup? | A provider-owned number fallback, tested while Abby is unavailable; block live-call readiness until proven. | Product and voice engineering; before live routing. |
| D-10 | What may Abby say first at each location? | Require a location-scoped greeting with recorded clinic approval and owner; block voice admission if absent, and never send it before session activation. The reference greeting is only a synthetic fixture. | Clinic owner; before live routing. |
| D-11 | Where may a standalone Cliniko clinic keep durable patient-specific booking and staff-follow-up details? | Cliniko only. Abby may persist patient phone numbers and Cliniko patient IDs in its lookup index, but no patient-specific action, task, review, or outcome details. If Cliniko cannot durably own and safely reconcile a request or staff item, that patient-specific capability is unavailable, including `requestOnly`; a generic non-patient alert or caller fallback is not a delivered staff task. Embedded buyers may use their own authorized patient/work system. | Decided by product owner; Cliniko workflow and failure handling must be proven under D-07 before patient-specific handoff. |

| D-12 | Which contract governs standalone call durability under D-06/D-11? | D-06/D-11 govern. Standalone Cliniko holds SAM and active-call ownership transiently; Abby does not persist a provider call ID, call receipt, replay key, presentation, call outcome, or patient-linked event. It refuses simultaneous duplicate streams across processes with a PostgreSQL session lock, but cannot promise replay refusal after all processes lose a call. Provider fallback on process loss must be proven with a real call before patient traffic. An embedded host may supply its own authorized durable call ledger and full FR-11 replay contract. | Decided by product owner 2026-09-30; live fallback proof remains open. |
| D-13 | Can voice engineering make real test calls before clinic readiness? | Only on a dedicated test number with an explicitly allowed presented caller number, signed Twilio webhook and stream, isolated test scope and database, demo data, and an independently configured number fallback. This temporary rehearsal path keeps general readiness unready and cannot authorize clinic or patient traffic. Caller ID is an allowlist clue, not identity proof. | Authorized by product owner request to prepare Twilio voice testing; real carrier results and clinic approval remain open. |

| D-14 | Who authenticates clinic administrators in standalone and embedded Abby? | Standalone Abby has its own sign-in behind a replaceable identity adapter. When embedded after acquisition, the buyer supplies its authenticated user, clinic membership and roles through that adapter; the core and admin commands keep their existing authorization and scope checks. Cliniko API credentials authorize the PMS connection and are not administrator login credentials. Standalone implementation uses Abby-owned email magic links with trusted preprovisioned membership; no external identity provider is required. The owner confirmed on 2026-10-03 that no email delivery service is selected. | Identity boundary decided by product owner 2026-10-02. Local magic-link HTTP, HTTPS browser and PostgreSQL proof and final release/parallel gates passed on source `b42b507`; synthetic delivery only. Real mail delivery, deployed TLS and clinic provisioning/recovery approval remain open. See `phase-2/STANDALONE_MAGIC_LINK_IDENTITY.md`. |

| D-15 | What evidence permits each standalone Cliniko write to be activated? | Apply the operation-specific [write activation contract](../phase-2/CLINIKO_WRITE_ACTIVATION.md). D-16 accepts best-effort standalone Cliniko: exact checks, one possible send, post-write inspection, no replay/compensation and proven Cliniko-owned review, with external-writer races explicitly accepted. The owning-diary embedded contract retains atomic target/exclusion/receipt guarantees. Current clinic/caller authority, informed confirmation, stop semantics, privacy, recovery/staff ownership and operation-specific E2Es remain required for both. All standalone write and patient-specific request-only modes stay unavailable until their remaining gates pass. | Revised following the product owner’s explicit 2026-10-04 selection of best-effort. C-LOCAL-53/54/55 remain bounded evidence; no provider atomicity or clinic approval is inferred. |
