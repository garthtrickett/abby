# Class movement staff resolution

Status: implementation in progress; no activation or completed gap 10 claim.

The live caller owner grants one separately confirmed phase at a time. A cold
staff reader cannot reconstruct that owner, renew caller consent, replay a leg
or promote an uncertain caller action to committed. Cliniko owns the review
and patient-linked attendance records; Abby adds no local recovery journal.

Staff resolution means an independently authorized observation of the current
joint native state after human investigation and sender quiescence. It is
separate from the original call outcome. A recalled review alone proves neither
quiescence nor completion. The trusted clinic host must authenticate the current
clinic administrator, check current directory membership and independently
permit the exact review and resolution before and after protected reads.

The request supplies a native review reference and may cite a replacement
attendance reference found during staff investigation. Neither is authority.
The module checks exact account, clinic, source/destination, staging policy,
terms, original attendance, replacement creation marker and case effects.
Ordinary/pass observations reuse the native readers and their complete-page,
fresh-proof and physical-budget checks. Unknown or contradictory records stay
for review. Changed policy/terms require fresh human handling in Cliniko. The initial
reader uses current captured contracts and upcoming class occurrences; expired
contracts and past occurrences stay for manual Cliniko investigation.

An active exact destination attendance may be discovered from native state.
For restoration, a cited cancelled replacement must match the original
creation marker, cancellation reason and unlinked state; absence from the
active roster alone cannot prove restoration. A pass also needs unchanged
original case usage. The reader makes no provider mutation or durable local
completion claim. Its public result contains only fixed observations and
whether the native review was acknowledged.

The dashboard route must be explicitly supplied by the authenticated owning
host, same-origin and clinic-admin only. Request JSON cannot supply actors,
patient proof, policies, terms, stage capabilities or a recovery permission.
Missing host provisioning leaves the route unavailable. Real clinic/operator
acceptance, broader cost cohorts and combined serving/privacy checks remain
separate qualification work.
