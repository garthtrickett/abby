| `booking.individual.success` | After verified authority, played confirmation, acknowledged Cliniko-owned review and preparation, final online/approved-roster availability precedes one possible create. Later diary/observer/speech failures cannot veto it. | [Host](../src/standalone/cliniko-booking-host.ts), [guard](../src/standalone/cliniko-booking-guard.ts), [offers](../src/standalone/cliniko-booking-offers.ts), [writer](../src/cliniko/booking-write.ts); [transport ordering](../test/cliniko/provider-acknowledged-booking.test.ts), [writer](../test/cliniko/booking-write.test.ts), [host](../test/cliniko/standalone-booking.test.ts). |
