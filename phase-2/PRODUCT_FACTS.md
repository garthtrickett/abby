# Implemented product facts for operators

**Verified:** 16 October 2026, staged movement foundation above the dispatch-lease and result-reader corrections (local prerequisite `cd9b4ca`). These facts describe inspected source and the synthetic checks recorded in [execution progress](EXECUTION_PROGRESS.md). They do not establish deployment, clinic approval or real-provider acceptance. This small reference implements audit R11 alongside the changed behavior; it is separate from public clinic FAQs and support advice.

| Fact | Implemented behavior | Source and witnesses |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `booking.dispatch.lease` | Individual online/roster and native class creation require the session-lease deadline observed during final preparation to remain live after the final read and at transport. An already observed renewal supplies its newer deadline; an unobserved renewal cannot extend that captured send window. No extra database read or timer follows final availability. This limits sending and does not add a post-acknowledgement success veto. | [Individual guard](../src/standalone/cliniko-booking-guard.ts), [class host](../src/standalone/cliniko-class-booking-host.ts); [actual SAM expiry/renewal boundaries](../test/cliniko/booking-dispatch-lease.test.ts), [individual ordering](../test/cliniko/provider-acknowledged-booking.test.ts), [class ordering](../test/cliniko/class-booking-acknowledgement.test.ts). |
| `booking.individual.success` | After verified authority, played confirmation, acknowledged Cliniko-owned review and preparation, final online/approved-roster availability precedes one possible create. Exact HTTP 201 acknowledgement, including patient/selection, full interval, marker and active state, establishes success. Later diary/observer/speech failures cannot veto it. | [Host](../src/standalone/cliniko-booking-host.ts), [guard](../src/standalone/cliniko-booking-guard.ts), [offers](../src/standalone/cliniko-booking-offers.ts), [writer](../src/cliniko/booking-write.ts); [transport ordering](../test/cliniko/provider-acknowledged-booking.test.ts), [writer](../test/cliniko/booking-write.test.ts), [host](../test/cliniko/standalone-booking.test.ts). |
| `booking.individual.unknown` | Headers, a body still being parsed, a lost/nonexact response or a later matching row do not establish this booking's success. No automatic create replay, cancellation/restoration or review closure follows uncertainty. Patient-specific recovery remains in Cliniko, with no new standalone local attempt/receipt queue. | [Host](../src/standalone/cliniko-booking-host.ts), [review runner](../src/standalone/cliniko-reviewed-send.ts); [process loss](../test/cliniko/standalone-booking-process.test.ts), [PostgreSQL/privacy](../test/cliniko/standalone-booking-postgres.test.ts). |
| `booking.individual.cancellation` | A validated booking acknowledgement is reported before asynchronous result delivery. The worker preserves only a matching booking reported during its active dispatch; late or foreign reports cannot overwrite a finished action. Other operations retain their own contracts. Ended calls and stale workers are not reopened. | [Host interface](../src/core/host.ts), [worker](../src/core/effect-worker.ts), [runtime](../src/core/runtime.ts), [transition](../src/core/transition.ts); [deadline cases](../test/text/effect-deadline.test.ts), [review cases](../test/cliniko/reviewed-send.test.ts). |
| `booking.class.success` | Ordinary and independently approved-pass creation finish permission, entitlement/history, proof, metadata, SAM/review and quota preparation before the final complete double capacity observation. Exact HTTP 201 attendee/self/patient/occurrence/marker/active-state and approved-case bindings establish creation. No post-create attendee/capacity/pass-use GET gates success. Later capacity, duplicate or consumption races cannot erase it; payment and atomic exclusion are not established. | [Class host](../src/standalone/cliniko-class-booking-host.ts), [dispatch reader](../src/cliniko/class-attendance-read.ts), [writer](../src/cliniko/class-attendance-write.ts); [exact acknowledgement and ordering](../test/cliniko/class-booking-acknowledgement.test.ts), [ordinary host](../test/cliniko/class-booking-host.test.ts), [pass host](../test/cliniko/class-pass-booking-host.test.ts), [compiled ordinary](../test/voice/class-runner.test.ts), [compiled pass](../test/voice/class-pass-runner.test.ts). |
| `booking.class.caseEvidence` | An ordinary response may omit the optional case property and acknowledge creation without proving it is unlinked. A returned unapproved case or undocumented null/empty shape stays nonexact. Approved-pass creation requires its exact case. A move requiring an unlinked replacement still needs native null-filter inspection; creation alone cannot authorize original cancellation. | [Writer and unlinked inspection](../src/cliniko/class-attendance-write.ts), [ADR 0002](../docs/adr/0002-provider-acknowledged-booking.md); [creation cases](../test/cliniko/class-booking-acknowledgement.test.ts), [staging cases](../test/cliniko/class-attendance-staging.test.ts). |
| `booking.class.recovery` | Lost/nonexact/unparsed responses remain uncertain and cannot replay. A known creation acknowledgement survives later host/review cancellation; an ended call remains ended. Cliniko-owned reviews remain discoverable after killed owners, including after acknowledgement before caller confirmation. Cold recovery makes no provider mutation or completion claim and stores no patient-bearing local attempt/receipt. | [Class host](../src/standalone/cliniko-class-booking-host.ts), [review runner](../src/standalone/cliniko-reviewed-send.ts); [acknowledgement/abort](../test/cliniko/class-booking-acknowledgement.test.ts), [actual process loss and PostgreSQL/backup privacy](../test/cliniko/class-booking-recovery-process.test.ts), [review cases](../test/cliniko/reviewed-send.test.ts). |
| `booking.scope` | Individual creation includes booking after separately proven registration. Native class creation includes an already approved case/pass. Class moves/pass relinking, cancellation, rescheduling, correction and resends retain operation-specific completion requirements; an owning diary retains atomic target/exclusion guarantees. | [Intake composition](../src/standalone/cliniko-intake-booking-session.ts), [class host](../src/standalone/cliniko-class-booking-host.ts), [ADR 0002](../docs/adr/0002-provider-acknowledged-booking.md); [intake cases](../test/cliniko/intake-booking-session.test.ts), [owning diary](../test/storage/owning-host-write-contract.test.ts). |
| `class.move.partialTruth` | The source foundation records inspected original/replacement attendance and case-association progress separately from whole-move completion. Each pending phase requires a distinct played readback and subsequent affirmation bound to current same-patient proof, the live session lease and independently unexpired movement terms. Ending, identity denial, interrupted playback and uncertain sends retire further authority while retaining observed effects. Public summaries omit private continuation/proof references; generic leg acknowledgements and a fresh host cannot establish whole-move completion. The native serving/renewal and operator recovery routes remain unfinished, so these contracts do not enable staged clinic moves. | [Progress](../src/core/class-movement-progress.ts), [receipts](../src/core/class-movement-continuation.ts), [consent](../src/core/class-movement-consent.ts), [transition](../src/core/transition.ts), [worker](../src/core/effect-worker.ts); [actual SAM, result-reader and fresh-host recovery contracts](../test/text/class-movement-sam.test.ts), [receipt contracts](../test/text/class-movement-receipt.test.ts), [consent contracts](../test/text/class-movement-consent.test.ts). |
