# Implemented product facts for operators

**Verified:** 16 October 2026, unpublished native movement cost and four-phase inspection refinement above qualified foundation `2593364` (draft PR #465). These facts describe inspected source and the synthetic checks recorded in [execution progress](EXECUTION_PROGRESS.md). They do not establish deployment, clinic approval or real-provider acceptance. This small reference implements audit R11 alongside the changed behavior; it is separate from public clinic FAQs and support advice.
