# Outbound contact review by clinic staff

**Owner decision, 22 October 2026:** Target clinic staff review first when private or unclear audio prevents a reliable whole-call opt-out result. Future outbound calls remain blocked while the contact result is unresolved. Whole-call recording and automated complete-negative classification are not selected by this decision.

**Delivery status:** This contract and its engineering are in progress. No staff-review workflow, native write, hold release or real clinic acceptance is established by this document. Existing positive-stop/DNC and independent provider-capacity owners retain their operation-specific contracts. The implementation must be qualified through actual serving, native records, PostgreSQL exclusions, cold discovery/process loss, full unfiltered privacy and review/CI before delivery.

## Meaning of a staff review request

A pending request says only that the clinic must resolve an outbound contact decision. It does not say no stop request occurred, that an opt-out was heard, that all audio was observed, that the provider call completely ended, or that another call is permitted. Staff must not infer missing private speech from absent recordings or a successful clinical booking. Clinical outcomes and ordinary administrative memos remain independently truthful.

Use clinic-approved contact-review storage, separately from financial reviews and administrative booking memos. Keep only the exact permitted source context in the clinic-owned record. A pending request binds the original possible-launch record and separately authenticated terminal-notification provenance. Staff obtain any patient/phone/provider context through the original request and attempt records under their own current grants; the pending record does not duplicate that private context. A completed notification remains notification provenance, never complete provider or contact truth.

One stable opaque purpose derives from the original possible-launch action, independently of policy, key and code revisions. A lost write acknowledgement cannot mint another purpose or authorize a second POST. A fresh permitted native history scan may discover the first record; absence or ambiguous/archived/edited history stays unresolved. No private local queue, payload or digest is introduced.

## Workflow to deliver

1. Retain a canonical pending-review request under independent current native type/content, original-source, notification/source, producer and retention grants. Recheck exact scope/account/patient/source across asynchronous quota/body waits and use the actual shared native180 budget.
2. Provide separately authorized current read and complete native history discovery. Show pending review in clinic-owned records without copying private content into local operational state. Known native ACK survives later reporting/grant failures; unknown remains unknown.
3. Connect the actual optional signed status/recovery path after restrictive positive-stop retention and terminal-notification retention. Native or provider uncertainty, missing grants, source drift, process loss and write response loss must preserve restrictive contact state. Default activation remains separate.
4. Keep future outbound contact blocked. A request acknowledgement, staff opening/closing a task or absent stop evidence cannot clear DNC or release a contact hold. Any later approved contact decision/reconsent uses its own exact current clinic authority and provenance; it never rewrites the original call into a complete no-stop result.
5. Qualify native exact receipts, malformed/foreign/ambiguous records, lost response and fresh process/restore discovery, shared budget/claims, revoked grants, positive-stop preservation and full-column/unfiltered backup privacy. Then finish operator facts, acceptance observations and normal delivery checks.

The earlier whole-call no-stop/all-ASR requirement is preserved as an unselected automation path. It does not mark that automation complete, relax positive-stop recognition, approve recording, grant reconsent, supply actual clinic/provider/acoustic/source/restore acceptance or activate outbound calls. The full execution-order scope continues with this explicit owner-selected workflow.
