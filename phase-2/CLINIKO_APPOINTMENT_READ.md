# Verified Cliniko appointment reads in synthetic calls

`ClinikoAppointmentReader` supplies the optional `AppointmentHost` port to the standalone and embedded compositions. It accepts a host-issued `verificationRef`, never a caller-provided Cliniko patient ID. The injected `PatientProofHost` must resolve one current patient within the bound tenant, clinic, and location. The adapter rejects missing, ambiguous, expired, foreign-scope, or non-Cliniko patient proof before making an HTTP request. Its own clock checks proof expiry and rejects caller or agent timestamps more than 30 seconds away. The reference host's proof method is a synthetic fixture, not an approved real-clinic verification policy.

After proof, the adapter requests the configured business time zone and a patient- and business-filtered appointment list over a bounded future range. It caps pages and response size, checks each returned patient and business link, rejects changing totals and malformed rows, and omits cancelled, archived, or deleted appointments. It projects only appointment references and start/end times. Provider notes and names never enter the result. It makes no appointment write and cannot authorize cancellation or rescheduling.

A final Abby caller turn or `appointments.read.v1` agent command uses the same `TextReception` and call transition. The transition rechecks scope, proof expiry, observation freshness, time zone, range, and row shape before committing a notice. It speaks at most three local-time appointments, creates a durable read receipt, and exposes only status, source, and count to agent progress and staff call review. A failed proof creates a verification follow-up without a Cliniko read or appointment disclosure. Provider and evidence failures create a system follow-up. A queued notice is not marked played until the attached voice session receives playback evidence.

`test/cliniko/appointments.test.ts` covers HTTP filters, proof-first denial, cross-scope rows, pagination faults, cancelled rows, and typed provider failures. `test/voice/appointment-read.test.ts` runs the adapter through final-turn, presentation, playback, failure, and agent-command paths with both memory and PostgreSQL ledgers. These are synthetic E2E scenarios contributing to `E2E-003` and `E2E-015`; they do not complete either scenario's real caller or approved host verification gate.

The appointment reader accepts the shared [Cliniko read budget](CLINIKO_READ_BUDGET.md) used by the other adapters for the same API user.

A controlled [demo-account probe](../phase-0/CLINIKO_EVIDENCE.md) now confirms one positive adapter read against an active synthetic appointment, the business time zone, and complete fixture cleanup. Before caller use, a clinic owner must approve a verification method and disclosure wording, shared-phone ambiguity must be tested, and real media E2E must prove interruption and completed playback. Until then, this adapter is opt-in for synthetic/test compositions only.
